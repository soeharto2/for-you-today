To My Friend, My Sunflower

I wanted to write you something real this year, not just a quick message between everything else. You deserve more than that, and honestly, I wanted to say a few things out loud.

Some people just make life better by being in it. You're one of them. You show up, you listen, you laugh at the right moments, and somehow you make hard days feel a little lighter.

What I love most is that you're completely yourself. No performance, no apology. Being around that makes it easier for the rest of us to be ourselves too.

I think about the little things: the inside jokes that make no sense to anyone else, the random messages that arrive at exactly the right time, the way you can turn an ordinary day into a good memory without even trying.

Even when life gets busy, or the distance gets in the way, I never doubt that you're in my corner. And I hope you never doubt that I'm in yours.

Friends like you are rare. Thank you for the laughs, the honesty, the support, and for being someone I can count on.

So today, I just want you to know something simple.

Happy birthday.

I hope today feels warm.

I hope it feels kind.

And I hope you feel how loved you are, today and every day. You're never celebrating alone.

With all my love and gratitude, Your friend
